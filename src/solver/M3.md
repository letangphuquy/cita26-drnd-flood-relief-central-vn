M3. Diverse Parent Selection
Idea: When selecting the second parent for crossover, require p2.X != p1.X. Retry tournament up to 3 times; fall back to normal if no different X found.

Why it works: Ensures crossover between different hub topologies, producing offspring that explore new X combinations. Without this, 2 dominant X-configs mostly cross with themselves → same X in children.

Complexity: ~5 lines around the tournament calls. Mild effect if M1 isn't active (new X still gets killed in survival).

M4. Individual Aging
Idea: Add an age field. Increment each generation an individual survives. In elitist selection, penalize old individuals (age > threshold) by reducing their effective crowding, or force-replace the oldest N% each generation.

Why it works: Forces population turnover, preventing any lineage from dominating indefinitely. Well-known in ALPS (Age-Layered Population Structure) literature.

Complexity: ~25 lines + new field in Individual. More invasive and harder to tune the age threshold. Risk of destroying good solutions.
