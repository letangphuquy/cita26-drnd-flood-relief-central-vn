Flow files in `flows/` were generated by `visualizer/preprocess_flows.py`
and cover all 12 Pareto solutions × 3 scenarios.

v2 uses pure Delaunay topology (~342 road edges). Non-adjacent pairs have
BIG_M road cost; water/air paths are BFS-extended through the same Delaunay
geometry. See `audit/doc_dataset_methodology.md` for full methodology.

**Note on Z1 vs v1:** v2 Z1 values (~38–92M) are larger than v1 (~12–16M).
Two factors: (1) Delaunay planar graph — ~342 direct edges replace K_n, so
multi-hop Dijkstra paths accumulate higher transport cost; (2) tier-based
hub capacities total ~0.73× max_demand, so the MCF must route significant
hub-to-hub transshipment flows to cover residual demand, adding transfer cost.

**Note on capacity design:** Hub kappa values are set to 0.73× max_demand
(total across all 20 hubs). Shortfalls at individual hubs are covered by
origin supply nodes and hub-to-hub MCF transshipment flows (inst.alpha
discount applied). This is by design — capacities represent pre-positioning
limits, not throughput ceilings.
