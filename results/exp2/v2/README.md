Flow files in `flows/` were generated by `visualizer/preprocess_flows.py`
and cover all 14 Pareto solutions × 3 scenarios.

v2 uses pure Delaunay topology (~342 road edges). Non-adjacent pairs have
BIG_M road cost; water/air paths are BFS-extended through the same Delaunay
geometry. See `audit/doc_dataset_methodology.md` for full methodology.

**Note on Z1 vs v1:** v2 Z1 values (~38–92M) are larger than v1 (~12–16M).
Two factors: (1) Delaunay planar graph — ~342 direct edges replace K_n, so
multi-hop Dijkstra paths accumulate higher transport cost; (2) tier-based
hub capacities total ~0.73× max_demand, so the MCF must route significant
hub-to-hub transshipment flows to cover residual demand, adding transfer cost.

**Note on capacity design:** Hub kappa values are set to ~0.73× Extreme-scenario
demand (total across all 20 hubs). Capacities are anchored to
`total_base_pop × GAMMA × max_sev_mult` — a scenario-independent planning
estimate — so kappa is a proper Stage-1 constant, not fitted to realized
scenario outcomes. Shortfalls at individual hubs are covered by origin supply
nodes and hub-to-hub MCF transshipment flows (inst.alpha discount applied).

**Note on seed:** seed=0 shows metaheuristic sensitivity to the population-anchored
kappas (converges to 0 feasible in 500 gens). seed=2 finds feasible solutions at
gen 100 and is used as the canonical result.
